Recording method

All MP4s are native 1920x1080 desktop-region recordings at 30 fps, with the
cursor captured, no system audio, no cuts, overlays, compositing, scaling or
speed changes. The desktop is 3440x1440; only the actual top-left 1920x1080
region was recorded. MKV captures were remuxed to MP4 with video stream copy.
validation.json confirms identical encoded video before/after remux, complete
error-free decoding and 30fps presentation timestamps rounded to the source
Matroska millisecond clock (33/34ms steps). These checks do not prove that the
operating system captured every unique refresh.

The real monitor runs nvidia-smi dmon -s um in successive 12-sample batches to
repeat its column headings. proof-gpu.log contains that output. The engine
uses sequential residency, safe weight copies and disabled intermediate node
cache. Native precision and attention kernels are unchanged. The product
video uses LTX-2.5 Q4_K_M; the image uses Z-Image Turbo BF16.

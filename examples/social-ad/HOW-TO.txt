Slow Mornings — editable portrait coffee ads

Extract this pack. In the local Aftertake Film editor, import the media/ files (three MP4s, a canvas PNG and the WAV voice recordings), then choose Import film project and project-a.json or project-b.json. Sources resolve by checksum, not by their original machine-specific asset IDs. Adjust text, timing, motion, voice or trims; then Export film. The main source snapshot includes the app.

The sourceFiles paths are relative to the full source checkout. The MP4 ad exports can be viewed directly. App code is MIT; media and model licenses apply separately. See ATTRIBUTION.txt and sources.json.
